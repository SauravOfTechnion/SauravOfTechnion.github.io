Trajectory & background

A little about my orbit.

From aerospace guidance laws to agentic AI — the same discipline, a wider flight envelope.

Violin, physical AI, machine learning, and reinforcement learning — one closed loop with different instruments.

I trained as an Aerospace Engineer at IIT Madras (B.Tech, AIR 2588) with a Data Analytics minor, then spent years building ADAS simulation and perception systems before moving into applied AI.

The through-line is guidance under uncertainty: path-following for UAVs, system-in-the-loop parking assists, real-time EO/IR tracking on Jetson Orin, and now LLM agents that reason over trust documents and mortgage workflows.

I play violin for the same reason I work on physical AI. Bow, string, and ear are a feedback loop you can hear. A policy on a robot is that loop with a body and a loss.

Machine learning and reinforcement learning are how I give those loops a memory — world models, CUDA, Dreamer, and reward models you can actually inspect.
